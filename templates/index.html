Why this matters

Indian farmers feed a population of roughly 1.4 billion, and pests alone cost an estimated ₹50,000 crore a year. Choosing a crop that suits the soil, and correcting nutrients rather than over-applying fertilizer, raises yield while slowing the soil degradation that imbalanced fertilization causes.

CropCraft is a decision aid, not a replacement for agronomic advice. Its accuracy on held-out data is published in the project README.
